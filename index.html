This tool estimates probability from the games you enter, assuming a roughly normal spread of outcomes. Small sample sizes (fewer than 5-6 games) make these estimates unreliable. It does not account for matchup, injury, weather, game script, or live sportsbook odds. Treat it as one input among many, not a prediction.
