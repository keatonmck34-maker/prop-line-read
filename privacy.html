Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The Green Room (“we”, “us”) is a sports statistics tool. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it. You can reach us at thegreenroomappofficial1@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Your password is handled by our login provider and stored in a protected (hashed) form; we cannot see it.
  • Sign-up confirmation: that you confirmed you are 18 or older and accepted our Terms, and when.
  • What you save: player names, bet types, lines, and game numbers you choose to save to your account.
  • Preferences: such as your chosen color theme.
  • Technical data: basic information such as IP address and browser type, which our hosting and database providers record in their logs.

How we use it

To run the service, keep your account secure, sync your saved items across devices, respond to your messages, and fix problems. We do not sell your information and we do not show ads. We do not currently use analytics tools; if that changes, we will update this page.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase provides login, our database, and the server code behind auto-fill.
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • GitHub hosts the public football data files our server reads. Your personal information is not sent there.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather forecasts. We send only a stadium location and date, never information about you.

Browser storage

The site stores your login session and theme choice in your browser so it works as expected. It does not use advertising cookies.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is open. To delete your account and saved data, email us and we will do so within 30 days.

Security

We use reputable providers and sensible safeguards, but no online service can promise perfect security.

Age

The Green Room is for people 18 and older. If we learn that someone younger has an account, we will delete it.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information. Email us and we will help.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
